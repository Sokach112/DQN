Paper2013 CNN, ReLU, MSE, vanilla target DQN, Adam; target copied every 2,500 updates. These are short pilots, not convergence benchmarks or exact historical-paper reproductions.

## Interpretation
Two tuning directions were tested independently: halving the learning rate and shortening epsilon decay. Three evaluations on one seed and a short budget cannot establish convergence or a reliable ranking. Loss/Q curves are logged single-minibatch measurements rather than averages across every update. Breakout scores use its own reward scale and should not be compared numerically with Pong scores.

## Remaining requirements
- Demonstrate sustained Pong improvement and the requested +21 evaluation score.
- Extend tuning comparisons with matched longer budgets and more seeds; explain reliable differences rather than minibatch noise.
- Reach comparable-to-paper performance in the additional game; running it is not sufficient.
- Optional softmax policy remains to be implemented or its omission justified under the epsilon-greedy allowance.
- Keep checkpoint/experiment metadata and configs with the report.

## Additional evaluation on new seeds

Each saved policy was tested on 10 complete games for each of reset seeds 100 and 200 (20 games total). Training remained seed 0; these are not independent training replications.

| Run | Seed 100 mean | Seed 200 mean | Combined mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | -21.00 | -21.00 | -21.00 |
| learning_rate | -20.90 | -20.60 | -20.75 |
| exploration | -19.60 | -19.90 | -19.75 |
| breakout | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 |

The faster-exploration Pong variant has the strongest score of these short pilots,
including the additional evaluations. A possible explanation is that its lower
epsilon collects more policy-driven trajectories; this is not a proven mechanism.
The learning-rate reduction yields a smaller difference, which could reflect
update stability or noise. Both explanations require repeat training seeds and
longer matched budgets. None of these results meet Pong +21. Breakout remains
unsuccessful, so the additional-environment performance requirement is incomplete.

## Follow-up: long-budget experiments (2026-10-18, later)

The four remaining requirements were carried forward with long runs and
multi-seed experiments; see
[`02_long_run_2026-10-18.md`](02_long_run_2026-10-18.md).
Key outcomes:

- Multi-seed tuning was repeated: 3 variants × 3 seeds × 200,000 decisions
  (800k frames). No tuning difference survives the seed spread at this budget,
  so the 20k-transition pilot ranking was noise.
- A 1.25M-decision (5M-frame) Pong run improved from −21 to a best periodic
  greedy evaluation of +6.0 and a best fresh-seed 20-game mean of +4.3. The
  required +21 was not reached; the measured paper-scale budget (2.5M
  decisions, larger replay, slower ε anneal) and exact commands are documented.
- Optional softmax action selection is implemented and tested; ε-greedy remains
  the justified default under the assignment's §2 allowance.
- A later paper-scale Kaggle run (2.5M decisions, one update per decision, replay
  200k, ε 1→0.1 over 250k) reached a 21.0 greedy evaluation at ten checkpoints;
  its saved checkpoint was independently verified winning 100/100 fresh games
  21–0. See [`03_pong21_2026-10-19.md`](03_pong21_2026-10-19.md).
- Breakout was not run at paper scale; a ready-to-run notebook (paper reference
  168/225) is at [`../kaggle/breakout_paper2013.ipynb`](../kaggle/breakout_paper2013.ipynb). The full test suite now
  has 26 tests, all passing.
